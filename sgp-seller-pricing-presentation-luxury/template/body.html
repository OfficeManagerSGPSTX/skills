Next steps

Preparing for market

A short list that moves the home toward the top of the range. Most of it is detail work and staging, not new projects.

Finish before photos
  • Front door, or the new handle you already have
  • Gate: if the gate and motor are on hand, install it if not too costly
  • Glass for the steam room
  • Blinds in the primary suite; temporary blinds down
  • Detail work to continue, room by room
  • Termite spot cleaned up downstairs
  • More gravel at the side and parking
  • AC unit repaired and stair lights connected
Staging
  • Theater and unfinished spaces virtually staged
  • Gym moves downstairs
  • Upstairs gym room becomes a lounge with the theater furniture, a TV on the wall and a coffee table
  • Sitting room off the primary staged as an office
  • Paint the rafters upstairs
  • Water features running for showings, or removed and the wall repaired
  • Landscaping left as is; sold with the garage and cottage plans
Paperwork
  • DPNR CZM permit or letter for the dock, with its permit number
  • Garage and cottage permit
  • Pre-listing inspection
  • Copy of the appraisal
  • Source for the cool pavers
